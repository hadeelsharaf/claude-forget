The re-install filter

After the detox, a plugin comes back only if it passes all four questions
1
Does it save more tokens than it spends?
Measured, not felt.
2
Does it work for the whole team?
Not only for me and my setup.
3
Does it follow the organization's rules as they are?
Not the rules I wish we had.
4
Do my own instructions still win over its instructions?
My CLAUDE.md stays in charge, or the plugin stays out.
Expected survivors: maybe a graph or memory layer, and one process skill for the team. Not much more.
