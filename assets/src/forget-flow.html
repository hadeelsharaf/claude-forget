How /forget stays reversible

Force-forget a discarded effort without ever deleting a byte
1
Find the memories about the concern
Index scan + keyword grep + semantic judgment over the current project's auto-memories.
2
Show the list — one confirm
You see exactly what will be buried before anything moves.
3
Bury, don't delete
Whole-match files move to memory/.trash/ and are renamed name.md → name.md.trashed; partial matches lose only the matching lines. The rename keeps trashed memories out of future sessions.
4
Record everything in the manifest
Every cut line is copied verbatim into .trash/TRASH.md before the cut, next to a restore recipe.
5
Re-index
MEMORY.md is rebuilt and dangling [[links]] are cleaned.
Restore = move the file back, drop the .trashed suffix, and paste the manifest's verbatim block back. Nothing is ever lost.
